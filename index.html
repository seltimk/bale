About Me

My name is Eric Seltim Katzing, a Software Developer, AI Enthusiast, Entrepreneur, and Public Speaker driven by innovation and problem-solving. I am passionate about building technology solutions that improve systems, empower people, and create real-world impact.

With a background in science and a growing focus on software development and artificial intelligence, I combine analytical thinking with practical creativity to develop modern digital solutions. My interests span software engineering, artificial intelligence, digital platforms, and technology-driven entrepreneurship.

I am also the co-founder of Marksel, an emerging marketplace platform designed to simplify buying and selling while enabling individuals and businesses to operate without the limitations of physical stores.

Beyond technology, I actively engage in leadership, research, and public speaking, using these platforms to share knowledge, inspire innovation, and promote meaningful change.
